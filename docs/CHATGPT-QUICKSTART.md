# Make your own counter in 6 steps

You do not need to know how to code. ChatGPT will help you build it.

## 1. Download the ZIP

[Click here to download](https://github.com/chowminyang/community-sales-counter/archive/refs/heads/main.zip). Find it in your computer’s **Downloads** folder. Keep it for step 4.

## 2. Check your ChatGPT account

Have a **ChatGPT Plus account with Sites access**. Sites lets ChatGPT put your app online. Check that you can use it before starting: [check availability here](https://help.openai.com/en/articles/20001339). A Plus subscription alone does not guarantee Sites is available in your account.

## 3. Make one product file

Open Word, Google Docs or a similar app. Put these details in one document and save it as a **Word file or PDF**:

- Your shop name and currency, such as Singapore dollars.
- For **each product**: paste its photo, type its name and add its selling price.
- List colours or sizes separately if you want separate buttons for them.
- Say how people can pay you, such as cash, bank transfer or coupons.

For example:

> **Shop:** Our Family Stall · **Currency:** Singapore dollars  
> **Product:** Blue bracelet  
> **Photo:** [Paste the blue bracelet photo here]  
> **Price:** $3.50

Repeat for every product. These are just examples—use your own photos and prices. Do not put your password in this file.

## 4. Give ChatGPT the files and this message

Start a new task in ChatGPT where **Sites** is available. Attach **both the ZIP and your product document**. Then copy and paste this:

> Please use this ZIP to create a sales counter for my shop on ChatGPT Sites. Use the photos, product names and prices in my attached document. Ask me if anything is missing.
>
> Create a new database for my shop so sales are saved when I close the app and can be seen on another device. Help me set my own password using a private password-entry screen, not in this chat. Follow the included deployment guide for the technical setup.
>
> I am a beginner. Do the technical work for me, show me a preview and help me test it before publishing. Explain any costs first. When I am happy with it, give me the website link to use on my tablet.

If ChatGPT cannot open the ZIP, ask it to guide you through opening the files. You do not need to edit the code yourself.

## 5. Choose your password and check the app

Follow ChatGPT’s instructions to **set your own password privately**. There is no existing password to use.

Check every photo, name and price. Then try a pretend sale: tap a product, record it as paid, and check **History**. Close and reopen the app to make sure the sale is still there. If using two devices, check it appears on both. Use **Undo** to remove that pretend sale.

Something wrong? Tell ChatGPT what happened and ask it to fix it before you start real sales.

## 6. Publish and use it

Tell ChatGPT:

> I am happy with the app. Please publish it and give me my website link. Make sure my password is required and my sales will be saved.

Open that link on your tablet or phone and bookmark it. Keep the app online. Tap **Paid** only after receiving payment. Do not use **Reset** unless you want to delete the saved sales.

To change products later, return to the same ChatGPT task, attach your updated product file and say: **“Update my products, but keep my existing sales.”**
